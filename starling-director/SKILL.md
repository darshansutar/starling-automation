# Starling Director

Apply [buffered timing and visual review](../starling-animation-pack/references/timing-and-visual-review.md) for storyboard/prompt timing, per-output visual checks, bounded repair and the user-owned manual lip-sync check.

Follow the [scene-matched reference selection](../starling-animation-pack/references/reference-bundle.md), including its failed-output repair exception.

Read [the handoff](../starling-prompt-pack/references/handoff-contract.md), [narration storyboard planning](../starling-animation-pack/references/narration-pairs.md) and [spatial fidelity](../starling-cinematography/references/spatial-fidelity.md). Own meaning, staging and action continuity; generation remains with production owners.

Map all semantic beats to source lines and measured audio. Specify visible people/objects, intention, starting state, required action and actual outcome. Preserve the full cast, branch differences and proposed-versus-completed work. Keep finalized-document content authoritative.

Plan two or three related shots per narration storyboard, each communicating source meaning through truthful staging and supported action. Match the below-picture action/camera labels to the planned shots and every measured hard cut. Quiet scenes use a gentle zoom or small pan while all subject/object states stay fixed; do not invent activity. Preserve time of day, sky, lighting and shadows unless the script explicitly requires a transition. Use the planned shots for meaningful related views; preserve complete source coverage. Include recognizable introductions, meaningful contextual B-roll and opening/true-ending coverage as production staging within canonical nodes. Preserve separate dialogue scenes and mixed-node order.

At image and prompt review, compare each actual composition and printed ACTION with its [detailed shot description](../starling-animation-pack/references/scene-preservation.md). Check that the starting pose, movement and outcome convey the source meaning without inventing activity or changing the approved staging. At video review inspect the complete action, reactions and sound, including intermediate motion. Reject unsupported additions or altered results. Panel Corrector owns detailed still repairs; Cinematography owns shot geometry. Missing inspection remains unverified.

Record director_review.json with exact source/output versions, panels or actual time ranges, observations, verdicts and minimum repair owner. A review invocation does not generate or submit repairs.

Follow [narration storyboard production](../starling-animation-pack/references/narration-pairs.md) for audio-led beat coverage, 1x2/1x3 selection, monochrome style fidelity, white action/camera label backgrounds, whole-second printed timings and active side-view staging without back shots.
