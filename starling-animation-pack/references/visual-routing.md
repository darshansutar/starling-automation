# Current visual routing

| Content | Images | EN2 inputs | Speech |
|---|---|---|---|
| Narration, filler, establishing and ending | One approved full-frame scene, or a labelled black-and-white 1x2/1x3 storyboard only when each cut has a distinct story purpose | Visible characters, environment, visible props, composition anchor last; Ultra quality=true | Accepted narration added during assembly |
| Dialogue | One approved clean full-frame scene derived from the scene master | Exactly that one scene ref plus approved ref_audios; Ultra quality=true | Existing speaker, turn and lip-sync workflow |

Follow [narration storyboard production](narration-pairs.md) and the [reference template](animation-template.md). Default to one continuous composition when a cut would only repeat or magnify the same subject. Use two or three scenes only when every cut reveals a new subject, action, evidence, location, time or reaction. Labels guide generation and remain outside the finished picture. Preserve canonical order in mixed nodes, accepted speech, original media and paid IDs. Reconcile versioned manifests before reuse; a skill update does not authorize new renders.
