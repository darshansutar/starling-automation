# Starling Cinematography

Read [visual routing](../starling-animation-pack/references/visual-routing.md), [two-shot boards](../starling-animation-pack/references/narration-pairs.md) and the authoritative [spatial fidelity checklist](references/spatial-fidelity.md). Own framing, geometry, depth and shot continuity. Preserve source meaning and approved design.

Record output aspect/dimensions and actual integer panel boxes. Every storyboard has two vertically stacked panels, each at output aspect, with no margins/gutters or printed planning content. Dialogue is a clean full-frame scene. Verify geometry on actual files.

Choose useful shot sizes/angles, clear introductions and readable relevant detail. Maintain room axis, eyelines, screen direction, proportional scale, depth ordering, movement clearance and supported body/object contact. Different viewpoints may change apparent size legitimately; compare actual reference geometry and perspective. Preserve coherent front/back object faces and occlusion.

Each camera stays fixed in position, angle, focal length and framing. Each board maps to two related full-screen shots with one measured hard cut. Narration continues across that cut. Put camera and cut timing in metadata/animation prompts. Give both shots enough measured time to read.

Review both still panels and their relationship, then complete animation for camera stability, timing, spatial continuity and physically plausible movement. Sampled frames cannot establish whole-clip correctness. Record cinematography_review.json with source/output hashes, dimensions, panel boxes, actual inspected intervals, findings and minimum corrections. Repairs return to the owning production skill.
